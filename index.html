LAUNDRY SABOTAGE

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Crew: Sort whites, darks and colors into the matching loads.
  2. Saboteur: Secretly slip mismatched clothing into the laundry.
  3. Crew players can Pull Item if something looks suspicious.
  4. Soap Save: The Crew gets one shared soap use per round. Use it on a contaminated item to clean it instantly and count it as a clean item.
  5. Crew wins by finishing 40 clean items.
  6. Saboteur wins by ruining 20 items.
  7. The round lasts 90 seconds. If time expires, the side with the higher percentage of its goal wins.
  8. Your role is secret until the round ends.
